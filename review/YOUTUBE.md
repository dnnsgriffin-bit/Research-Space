## Description

> Training was completed. Did work change?
>
> Evidence Studio is a prototype decision-support tool for learning and development teams. You tick the training measures you actually have, a seven-link evidence chain shows how far an honest claim can reach, you check four workplace conditions, and you leave with a one-page brief: what you can say, what you can't say yet, and what to measure next.

## LinkedIn

- Suggested first line: “Completion is a measure. It isn't evidence that work changed.”
- Call it a prototype, and say that the inputs in the film are examples.
