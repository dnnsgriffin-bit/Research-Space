# Evidence Studio: demo and portfolio kit

Use the live product link: https://evidence-studio-lab.lovable.app/ . For a direct case link, use https://evidence-studio-lab.lovable.app/example . Northstar Services is fictional; never present the sample as an employer engagement or measured impact.

## The simplest production path

Record the real site on a desktop at 16:9. Capture your screen and your own voice in Google Vids, trim pauses, add short captions, and export. Make a separate vertical crop for LinkedIn if desired; keep the evidence chain and inspector legible. Use one quiet transition between scenes. No avatar is needed. If you use Veo or Flow, limit it to a clearly illustrative five-second opening and cut immediately to the actual product. Never generate a fake testimonial, dashboard result, or Northstar staff member.

## Portfolio walkthrough: about 2 minutes

1. **0–20s — Context.** On the landing page, say: “I wanted a tool that helps leaders slow down at the right point: before a training request turns into an unsupported scale decision.”
2. **20–40s — Entry.** Open Northstar and say: “I made the worked example the front door. Visitors can follow a complete fictional case without registering or filling out a form.”
3. **40–75s — Signature interaction.** Select Intervention, Learning and Transfer. Say: “Every link has a state and an inspector: what is known, where it came from, what it does not establish, and what to collect next. Color reinforces the state, but the label carries the meaning.”
4. **75–100s — Challenge.** Open Skeptical Review. Say: “The product does not only assemble a recommendation. It pushes back with alternative explanations, context constraints, and the cheapest useful check.”
5. **100–120s — Output.** Show the decision brief. Say: “The output separates a rule-based Studio suggestion from a practitioner-entered decision. It also names the evidence basis and limits. The goal is a better decision, not a prettier claim.”

End with the live app URL and “Created by Dennis Griffin, Ed.D.”

Lovable credits are not needed to edit the source code locally. Verify the public URL after publishing before sharing it on LinkedIn.
